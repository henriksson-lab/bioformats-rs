## Interface follow-up

The flattened-series and per-resolution metadata gaps found during this replay
were subsequently fixed; see [pyramid/CZI verification](gap-pyramids-2026-10-26.md).

`axis_orientations()` returns five optional typed values for the selected
series. It preserves Inveon's upstream `indexOf("Z") - 2` placement (slot 0 for
XYZCT), even though that differs from the interface's documented axis order.
The combined Analyze/NIfTI reader keeps its existing Rust dimension order.
No pixel axes are reordered by orientation metadata.
